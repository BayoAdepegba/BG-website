About

Meet Coach BG

Coach BG with Carson Edwards — UPenn '29

Dedicated to developing the next generation of track & field athletes.

Coach Brenen Garrett — known as Coach B or CoachBG — leads OCSpeed Powered By Laurel Boys & Girls Club, a youth athletics program focused on universal speed development. Through structured training, discipline, and mentorship, Coach BG helps athletes of all ages build not just speed, but confidence and character.

Track is an excellent opportunity to experience many of the things that can help an individual mature as a person. Through adversity, as well as victory, student athletes challenge themselves and their teammates to achieve goals — learning to respect one another, themselves, and reach their fullest potential.

Practice & Requirements

Practice Schedule

Tuesday 6:15 PM — Track practice, swimming, road running
Thursday 6:15 PM — Track practice, plyometrics/hills

Location: Laurel High School or Boys & Girls Club (TBA)

Team Colors: Navy Blue, Grey, White — bring all uniforms/gear to meets!

Athlete Requirements

  • Academics First

    Maintain a 2.0 or higher GPA to compete on the team.

  • Attendance

    Attend ALL practice sessions and competitions. Notify coaches by 2:00 PM if you cannot attend.

  • Uniform & Gear

    Warm-up (pants + jacket) over workout clothing. Proper training shoes required — regular play shoes are NOT recommended.

  • Representation

    On and off the track, athletes represent OCSpeed and themselves. Maintain loyalty toward peers and respect toward adults.

  • Medical

    Make sure coaches know about any medical issues. Report any pain or injuries immediately.

Parent Expectations

  • Drop-Off

    Drop kids off on time. Walk younger kids down to the track area.

  • Hydration

    Provide your kids with enough water for practices and meets.

  • Observe From a Distance

    Please watch practice from a distance — no extra coaching. Ask questions before or after practice.

  • Focus on Development

    Focus on short/long-term goals, not rivalries. Create a loving atmosphere and allow athletes to set goals.

  • Your Attitude Matters

    As a parent, your attitude towards sports and competition will directly affect your athlete.

High School Athletes

College Recruitment

Coach BG helps student athletes navigate the path to competing at the next level.

1

Run the Times

Hit competitive marks in your events. Train hard, compete consistently, and build a strong performance record.

2

NCAA Eligibility Center

Sign up for the NCAA Eligibility Center to participate and receive scholarship funds for college.

3

Performance Profile

Create a professional performance profile letter with your PRs, accomplishments, and season highlights.

4

Reach Out to Coaches

Research D1 & D2 programs and email your profile to every coach at schools you'd like to attend.

5

Fill Out Questionnaires

Complete 5 college questionnaires every 2 weeks. Let coaches know which schools contact you.

6

Get Support

Let Coach BG know if you need college assistance — we can help schedule recruiting visits and connect you with programs.

Helpful Resources

High School Athletes: Speak with your guidance counselor about your four-year scheduling plan that meets NCAA requirements. Stay on top of your grades, clean up your social media, and try out for your high school or youth team. Youth athletes must keep a mental journal each month expressing long-term and short-term goals.

Nutrition

Fuel Your Performance

What you eat matters. Follow these guidelines to support your training.

Daily Plate

  • Whole Grains: 6 oz.
  • Vegetables: 2.5 cups
  • Fruit: 2 cups
  • Dairy: 3 cups
  • Protein: 5.5 oz.
  • Water: 8 cups minimum

Oils, solid fats, and sugars are not food groups — use rarely.

Athlete Tips

  • Rest is especially important
  • Mental health is important
  • Social life is important
  • Massages & flexibility are important
  • Set reasonable goals for yourself
  • Make permanent but gradual changes
  • Eat raw fruits and veggies
  • Purchase frozen goods instead of canned
  • Purchase alkaline water
